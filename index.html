Employee IT FAQs

Find anwers to common IT questions.

What are the IT support operating hours?

Self-service guides are available 24/7. The IT service desk is available Monday through Friday, 08:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time.

Do I need to talk to an agent if I need a new hardware replacement?

No, you can also open a service request ticket from the portal and include your employee ID, location and the reason for the request.

What should I do if I experience a network or VPN outage?

Check the known outages page first, then contact IT support directly if the issue is blocking critical work.
